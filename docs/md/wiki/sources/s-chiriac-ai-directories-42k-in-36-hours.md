# Sergiu（@s_chiriac）：「Directories Are Dead」？36 小时 $42K 不同意——三个自建目录案例

> 作者反驳「目录站就是垃圾、浪费时间、不会带来销售或流量」的主流说法，并给出本周三个实例：@botdirectoryai 被 @elonmusk 提及后，Elie Steinbock（@elie2222）那条推拿到 10m views，他随即把赞助价从 $800 涨到 $1200/mo、并表示随位置减少还会继续涨、listing 免费，如今每个广告位至少 $1K/month。Jonathan Wilke（@jonathan_wilke）的 outbid.lol 在上线 36 小时后报出 $42k revenue、最高出价 $10k（@lewiscarhart）、1,061,848 total visitors、以及至少 10 个抄袭者，中途还把 analytics 跑爆而改用 @DataFast_；他是花几小时在做晚饭时建好并发出去，同时全程 build in public。Rob Hallam（@robj3d3）的 canivibecodeit.com 走同样路线，现在从广告上赚 $9K/month。作者指出好目录 listing 的收益远不止流量：来自真实外链的 DR 提升、买家本来就在看的线上曝光、以及 LLM 日益靠读目录来回答「what’s the best tool for X」而产生的 AI 引用；他反问为什么会为一个位置花 $10K——在那个价位买的不是外链而是可见度。落点是：本篇三个案例都不是继续往目录投稿，而是自己造了一个目录，三人起步时都没有许可、团队或大受众，而你最熟的那个细分领域大概还没有属于它的目录。

That’s what they want you to believe.

Directories are spam. Directories are a waste of your time. You won’t make sales, you won’t get traffic, and nobody will ever find your business through one.

Meanwhile, this week alone:

## @elie2222 got a shoutout from the boss himself

[@botdirectoryai](https://x.com/botdirectoryai) got mentioned by @elonmusk.

> Quoted tweet: Elie Steinbock (@elie2222)

> Omg. 10m views for the Elon tweet about @BotDirectoryAI. Just increased sponsorship prices from $800 to $1200/mo. Will increase as more spots get taken. Free to list your bots! https://t.co/1mPcIZTr2z

Elie was already doing well with his marketing, but after that mention the traffic exploded. And let me be clear: it’s a genuinely good directory. You go there to find Grok bot setups and prompts you can actually use to improve your business, your workflow, or just about anything.

Today he’s charging at least $1K/month per ad spot.

## Then came the one that blew me away 🤯

[outbid.lol](https://outbid.lol/) by @jonathan_wilke.

> Quoted tweet: Jonathan Wilke (@jonathan_wilke)

> Good morning friends! Quick update on https://t.co/3tMzhIlC1s after 36 hours since launch: - $42k revenue - highest bid at $10k (@lewiscarhart you are crazy 🫶🏼) - broke my analytics and switched to @DataFast_ - 1,061,848 total visitors - at least 10 copycats so far

I’ve known Jonathan for a while now from Superstarter — I helped him with the Directories launch on it. But the way he executed this one deserves an award.

He built and launched it in a few hours. While making dinner.

36 hours later:

- $42K in revenue
- $10K highest bid
- 1,061,848 total visitors
- at least 10 copycats

ps: this amount is rising as we speak!

And the people buying spots are getting real traffic to their pages out of it. A simple idea, executed fast, paid exactly what it deserved.

But here’s the part I think most people missed: he documented the whole thing while he built it.

That’s build in public done properly. Not a screenshot of an MRR chart three months after the fact — the actual process, in real time, while it was still uncertain whether any of it would work.

So that’s two things they told you were dead.

## And let’s not forget

[canivibecodeit.com](https://canivibecodeit.com/) by @robj3d3 blew up the same way when it launched. People loved it.

> Quoted tweet: Rob Hallam (@robj3d3)

> GitHub hosts your code. Nowhere hosts your prompts. I built https://t.co/g7Ciu1sEzp: post what you vibecoded, with the exact prompts that made it. 1. Post your build 2. Paste the prompts you actually used 3. It’s live Everyone says “I built this in one prompt”. Here, you show ...

The concept is simple and kind of genius: you copy prompts to vibecode a clone of any service — Granola, Wispr, whatever you want.

Rob now makes $9K/month from ads on it.

## This isn’t three lucky flukes

TrustMRR. TAAFT. Product Hunt. Uneed. Submitting to good directories works — and traffic is only one of the payoffs.

Even when a listing doesn’t send you visitors, you can walk away with:

- a DR bump from a real backlink
- online visibility in places your buyers already look
- AI mentions, because LLMs are increasingly reading directories to answer “what’s the best tool for X”

And when the return is direct enough, people pay accordingly. Why do you think @lewiscarhart from Comp AI or @tibo_maker from Outrank put $10K into a single spot?

Nobody spends that on a whim. That’s not a backlink play — at that number you’re buying visibility.

I’m not guessing here. For more than two years I’ve been curating these directories at aidirectori.es and helping founders get listed on them, because I’ve seen what the right listing does.

## So here’s the actual lesson

The right directory can be the best distribution channel you get all year.

Then do the harder part.

Every example in this article is someone who stopped submitting to directories and built one instead. Elie, Jonathan, Rob — none of them had permission, a team, or a big audience on day one. Jonathan had a few free hours and a stove.

The niche you know best probably doesn’t have its directory yet.

Go build it.
