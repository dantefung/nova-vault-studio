# Khanh Nguyen（@khanhnqc）：10 个真实产品从 $0 到第一个 $1K——只收录叫得出产品名的案例

> 作者正准备从 $0 做一个新产品，于是翻了大量 X 帖子、只保留能叫出产品名的案例，并说明目标定为第一个 $1K 而不是 $10K 是因为他自己在 $0。10 个案例分别是：@everestchris6 的 Unloopa（把 AI 网站卖给本地商家），一篇不带链接不带推销的 Reddit 长帖拿到 1.1M views，24 小时做到 $1K MRR；@heyIrfan 的 Nodott 线索挖掘工具，广告 $0，反复改写私信直到回复率到 20-25%，4 个月 $500 MRR、35 个客户；@Orelzman 的 WriteStack，先前失败 12 个产品、在 $0 上坐了 600 天，把自己 Substack 做到 700 订阅时才开付费墙；@zuess05 的 Cubix Capture 录屏工具，改私信已在互动的人，约 5 个客户来自私信；@yusukelp 的 LandingBoost，$5 一次性扫描加 $35 终身价，约 200 天做到累计 $1K 营收；@JaniBangiev 的 Picmim 七个月到 €1K MRR，在广告和 UGC 创作者上花了约 €4K 几乎没拿到客户；@virgilerietsch 的 BlitzReels 在 13 个月 $0 之后一周做到 $300 MRR；@BrendanGoforth 的 Codex Heim 软上线一个月即盈利，98% 的营销是 TikTok/Reels/Shorts 自然短视频；@TusharKapil003 的 Reachlee 在产品还没跑通时就先做 SEO，首月 700+ 用户、$60 MRR；@ElitzaVasileva 的 own.page 抓住竞品 Bento 关停写了一篇博文，一周新增 6 个付费客户，freemium 约 1% 转化。他归纳的共性是：一对一沟通出现频率最高，10 个里有 3 个的付费用户来自 ChatGPT 回答，付费广告要在基础项改好之后才有效，许多人削减了免费额度但也有人给整月免费试用所以没有统一规则，以及好几位先在 $0 上待了很久——600 天、13 个月、7 个月。

I’m starting a new product from $0, and I wanted to see how other founders actually got their first customers. I went through a lot of posts on X and kept only the ones that name the product.

Why $1K and not $10K? I’m at $0. The first $1K feels like the right goal for now.

10 founders, 10 products, different channels. Here’s what each one did, in their own numbers.

1. @everestchris6: $1K MRR in 24 hours

来源：https://x.com/everestchris6/status/2028178013125542092

Unloopa, a tool for selling AI websites to local businesses.

One Reddit post walking through his whole manual process. No link, no pitch. 1.1M views. People DM’d him, and he sent back a free guide with one line about the product at the end.

2. @heyIrfan: $500 MRR, 35 customers in 4 months

来源：https://x.com/heyIrfan/status/2098403364434546804

Nodott, a lead-finding tool.

$0 on ads. Kept rewriting his DMs until the reply rate hit 20-25%, then got on calls and focused on understanding the problem instead of selling right away.

3. @Orelzman: $0 to ~$1K MRR after 600 days at $0

来源：https://x.com/Orelzman/status/2075229436002054539

WriteStack, a tool for writing and scheduling Substack Notes.

Failed 12 products first. Built a rough version fast, then DM’d people one by one for feedback. Listened instead of defending his UI (renamed one button, clicks went way up). Pivoted from an article generator to Notes after users said writing wasn’t their problem. Turned on the paywall when his own Substack had 700 subscribers, with a founder discount for early users.

4. @zuess05: First 10 paying customers

来源：https://x.com/zuess05/status/2069105266126393494

Cubix Capture, a screen recorder.

Posting on X got likes but almost no users. So he DM’d people already engaging with his posts, chatted first, pitched later. Took the harsh feedback and shipped updates almost daily. ~5 customers came from that, 3 from a Medium article, a couple from ChatGPT recommendations.

5. @yusukelp: First $1K in total revenue (not MRR) in ~200 days

来源：https://x.com/yusukelp/status/2069057644955078915

LandingBoost, a landing page audit that tells you the one thing to fix next.

Useful replies to people with real landing page pain (10-20 good ones > 100 empty ones). A $5 one-time scan + $35 lifetime deal with a visible “spots left” meter. Cut back the free stuff. A direct “~10 spots left” post with ~100 views still sold 2.

6. @JaniBangiev: 7 months to €1K MRR

来源：https://x.com/JaniBangiev/status/2108547425170235399

Picmim, an AI social media manager for small businesses.

Spent ~€4K on ads and UGC creators and got almost no customers. What finally worked: going after small businesses, selling outcomes instead of tech, a new onboarding, and a new, tighter Meta Ads campaign.

7. @virgilerietsch: $300 MRR in a week after 13 months at $0

来源：https://x.com/virgilerietsch/status/2080255764866707763

BlitzReels, an AI agent that turns long videos into branded clips.

Ran 3 experiments at once: a Meta Ads campaign, a stricter free tier (users upgraded after it), and a new channel. In the replies he says sales came from ChatGPT and Meta Ads. Spent ~€2,000 that month, not profitable yet.

8. @BrendanGoforth: Profitable 1 month after a soft launch (~$420 MRR if trials convert)

来源：https://x.com/BrendanGoforth/status/2086175685765177421

Codex Heim, a writing + worldbuilding app for fantasy authors.

Built it for his own fantasy book. 98% of his marketing was organic short-form video on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. Zero ads, zero UGC.

9. @TusharKapil003: 700+ users and $60 MRR in month one

来源：https://x.com/TusharKapil003/status/2084568724632433007

Reachlee, an Instagram growth tool for creators and businesses.

Started SEO before the product even worked, publishing blogs while the app was still broken. Optimized for AI answers too (use case pages, listicles, schema), and his first paying customer came from a ChatGPT answer. Priced by country. Meta and Google Ads brought volume but zero sales, so he used that traffic to fix onboarding.

10. @ElitzaVasileva: Past $100 MRR, then $172

来源：https://x.com/ElitzaVasileva/status/2023829317885546660

own.page, a link-in-bio / personal website builder.

When competitor Bento shut down, she wrote one blog post about it that caught people searching for alternatives, and DM’d a few people about the shutdown. Creators with big followings (one with 500K+) put own.page in their bios, which pulled in more users. 6 new paying customers in one week (freemium, ~1% conversion).

What I noticed:

- Talking to people one by one showed up the most: DMs, calls, replies to people with the exact problem.

- ChatGPT is quietly sending customers. 3 of these 10 got paying users from AI answers.

- Paid ads only worked after the basics changed. Picmim and BlitzReels burned money first, and Reachlee’s ads brought zero sales.

- A lot of them cut back the free stuff. But one gave a full month free trial, so there’s no single rule.

- Several sat at $0 for a long time first: 600 days, 13 months, 7 months.

I’ll try a few of these myself and share my real process here, results included.

Which one would you try first?
